Overview

Eboncrest is a Gothic dark fantasy text-based stat management game about the journey of Adrian, a young man faced with an unbridled debt too great to ever repay, by climbing the cursed mountain of Eboncrest, he seeks a way to beat his fate and save his siblings from imprisonment and abandonment. Progression happens in this game through events where the player first reads the description and choices presented while looking at illustrations of the scenes.
